Question 1

Consider the following autonomous first order differential equation.

\[\frac{dy}{dx}=(y-2)(y+7)^2.\]

The critical points are \(y=2\) and \(y=-7\). Classify these critical points (in the given order) as asymptotically stable, unstable, or semi-stable.

(A) asymptotically stable and asymptotically stable (B) asymptotically stable and semi-stable
(C) semi-stable and unstable (D) unstable and asymptotically stable
(E) semi-stable and semi-stable (F) asymptotically stable and unstable
(G) semi-stable and asymptotically stable (H) unstable and unstable
(I) unstable and semi-stable
Show answer
  1. unstable and semi-stable.

Question 2

Solve the following initial value problem.

\[\cos^2 x\sin x\frac{dy}{dx}+(\cos^3 x)y=4, \qquad y(\pi/3)=3.\]

Show answer

\[y(x)=\frac{4\tan x-\frac52\sqrt3}{\sin x}.\]

Question 3

Consider the differential equation

\[(4xy^2+3y+Ax^2y)+(4x^2y+Bx+2x^3)\frac{dy}{dx}=0.\]

  1. Find the values of \(A\) and \(B\) which make this differential equation exact. Enter the values of \(A\) and \(B\) (in that order), separated with a comma.

  2. Consider the differential equation above with \(A\) and \(B\) replaced by the values found in part (a). If the solution of the differential equation above satisfying the initial condition \(y(1)=1\) is written in implicit form as the equation \(f(x,y)=7\), compute the function \(f(x,y)\).

Show answer

  1. \(A=6\), \(B=3\).

  2. \(f(x,y)=2x^2y^2+3xy+2x^3y\).

Question 4

If the solution of the initial value problem

\[(3x^2+y)\,dx+(x^2y-x)\,dy=0,\qquad y(2)=12\]

is written in implicit form as the equation \(F(x,y)=72\), compute the function \(F(x,y)\).

Hint: Find an integrating factor.

Show answer

\[F(x,y)=\frac{y^2}{2}-\frac{y}{x}+3x.\]

Question 5

Consider the differential equation

\[(6x^n+2xy)y'=3x^n+6xy+2y^n.\]

  1. For which value of \(n\) is the first-order (nonlinear) differential equation above homogeneous?

  2. For the value of \(n\) found in part (a), use the method of homogeneous equations to find in explicit form the unique solution of the differential equation above that satisfies the initial condition \(y(1)=-6\).

Show answer

  1. \(n=2\).

  2. \(y(x)=-3x-\sqrt{9x^2+x^2\ln(x^3)}\).

Question 6

Solve the initial-value problem

\[8y'+(1+3x^2)y=\frac{e^{-x^3}}{y^7},\qquad y(0)=1.\]

Note that this is a Bernoulli differential equation.

Show answer

\[y(x)=e^{-x^3/8}.\]

Question 7

Solve the following differential equation,

\[(2tx+3t^2)x'=x^2,\]

with initial conditions

  1. \(x(1)=5\);

  2. \(x(1)=-3\).

Show answer

  1. \(x(t)=\frac12\left(-3t+\sqrt{9t^2+160t}\right)\).

  2. \(x(t)=-3t\).

Question 8

Solve the following initial value problem.

\[tx'-(8+t)x=t^{-7}x^2,\qquad x(1)=\frac15.\]

Show answer

\[x(t)=\frac{t^8}{-1+6e^{1-t}}.\]

Question 1

Using the fact that \(x_1(t)=e^t\) is solution of the second order linear homogeneous DE

\[(7+3t)x''-3x'-(4+3t)x=0,\]

find a second linearly independent solution \(x_2(t)\) using the method of reduction of order and then find the unique solution of the above DE satisfying the initial conditions

\[x(0)=-13,\qquad x'(0)=15.\]

Do NOT enter \(x(t)=\) as part of your answer.

Show answer

A second independent solution is \(x_2(t)=(17+6t)e^{-t}\). The IVP solution is \[x(t)=4e^t-(17+6t)e^{-t}.\]

Question 2

Solve the initial value problem,

\[3x^2y''-9x(x+1)y'+\left(\frac{27}{2}x+\frac{45}{4}\right)y =3x^{7/2}e^{6x},\]

\[y(1)=\frac1{18}e^6-e^3-1,\]

\[y'(1)=\frac5{12}e^6-\frac92e^3-\frac32,\]

given that \(y_1(x)=x^{3/2}\) solves the associated homogeneous differential equation.

Show answer

\[y(x)=x^{3/2}\left(\frac1{18}e^{6x}-e^{3x}-1\right),\qquad x>0.\]

Question 3

The weight is released from 7 feet above the equilibrium position with a downward velocity of 30 ft/s.

  1. Determine the time (in seconds) at which the mass passes through the equilibrium position. Round your answer to 4 decimals.

  2. Find the time (in seconds) at which the mass attains its extreme displacement from the equilibrium position. Round your answer to 4 decimals.

Show answer

  1. \(0.3605\) s.

  2. \(1.0219\) s, the turning point after release.

Question 5

Find the inverse Laplace transform of the following expression.

\[\frac{s-9}{(s+3)(s^2+4)}\]

Show answer

\[f(t)=-\frac{23}{26}\sin(2t)+\frac{12}{13}\bigl(\cos(2t)-e^{-3t}\bigr).\]

Question 6

Let \(y(t)\) be the solution to the following initial value problem.

\[y''+2y=e^{5t},\qquad y(0)=9,\quad y'(0)=6\]

Find \(Y(s)\), the Laplace transform of \(y(t)\).

Show answer

\[Y(s)=\frac{1}{s^2+2}\left(\frac{1}{s-5}+9s+6\right),\qquad s>5.\]

Question 8

Question 9

A mass attached to a spring, set in a container of molasses (a thick liquid) acts like a damped simple harmonic oscillator. The mass is 64 kg, the spring has a spring constant of 4 N/m and the molasses provides a damping force of 32 Ns/m.

  1. The system is

(A) underdamped
(B) overdamped
(C) critically damped

  1. A force of \(F(t)=-6e^{-t/4}\) (in N) is applied to the mass. If the method of undetermined coefficients is used to find a particular solution, which of the following is the best choice as a trial solution?

(A) \(At^2e^{-t/4}\)
(B) \((At^2+Bt+C)e^{-t/4}\)
(C) \((At+B)e^{-t/4}\)
(D) \(Ae^{-t/4}\)
(E) \(A\sinh(t/4)+B\cosh(t/4)\)
(F) \((At^2+Bt)e^{-t/4}\)
(G) \(At\sinh(t/4)+Bt\cosh(t/4)\)
(H) \(Ate^{-t/4}\)

  1. Initially, at \(t=0\)s, our mass is moving at a speed of 4 m/s as it passes through its equilibrium position (\(x=0\)m). If we are still under the force from part (b), at what positive time, in seconds, does it again pass through \(x=0\)m? (If it never does, type dne).
Show answer

  1. C. Critically damped.

  2. A. \(At^2e^{-t/4}\).

  3. The speed alone does not specify the direction. With initial velocity \(x'(0)=4\) m/s, the mass crosses again at \(t=256/3\) s. With \(x'(0)=-4\) m/s, there is no second crossing. In both cases \[x(t)=e^{-t/4}\left(x'(0)t-\frac{3}{64}t^2\right).\]

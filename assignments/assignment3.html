Question 1

  1. Every linear \(n\)th order homogeneous ODE has a solution of the form \(y(x)=e^{rx}\), where \(r\) is some real number.

  3. The fundamental set of solutions to \(y''-2my'+m^2y=0\), where \(m>0\), is given by \(\{e^{r_1x},e^{r_2x}\}\) for some appropriate real values \(r_1,r_2>0\).

  4. The auxiliary equation of \(ny''-my'+ky=0\), where \(n,m,k\) are real numbers with \(n\ne0\), is given by \(nr^2-mr+kr=0\).

  5. Two complex values that are not real solve the auxiliary equation of \(y''+my'+m^2y=0\), where \(m>0\).

Determine which of the above statements are True (1) or False (2).
